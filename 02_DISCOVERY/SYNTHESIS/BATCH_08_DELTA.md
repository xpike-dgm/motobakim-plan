## 1. Confirmed product rules added by Batch 08

### Entitlement vs historical trust
- Free entitlement siniri historical truth'u retroactive kilitlememelidir.
- Inactive motorcycle history, evidence, provenance, basic search, export, correction/dispute visibility ve own-evidence withdrawal erisimini korur.
- Payment/entitlement state ayni historical record'in trust level/source/evidence/correction/dispute bilgisini azaltamaz.
- Historical trust maintenance yeni premium activity sayilmaz: typo/error correction, dispute response, own-evidence withdrawal ve existing claim'i supporting/correcting evidence ile guclendirme free kalmalidir.
- Inactive motorcycle'da unrelated new routine note/work yeni activity sayilabilir.

### Active vs inactive motorcycle lifecycle
- Current working free proposal tek active motorcycle'a izin verir; user active motorcycle'i degistirebilir.
- Inactive motorcycle routine maintenance reminders susabilir.
- Past work'u etkileyen critical safety correction/recheck reach-back entitlement'tan bagimsiz devam eder.
- Inactive motorcycle safety reach-back yalniz alert degildir; required recheck ve safe-recovery guidance da accessible kalmalidir.
- Reactivation old history/evidence'i geri getirir; reminders current mileage/use context ile yeniden degerlendirilir.
- Interrupted physical work old confirmations'a dayanarak automatic resume edilmez.

### Selected full-guide lifecycle
- Current working free proposal account-level tek selected full-guide entitlement'tir.
- User selection oncesi motorcycle fit ve scope'u anlayabilmelidir.
- Bike switch veya guide completion yeni free guide hakkini automatic uretmez.
- Selected guide yeniden kullanilabilir ve future corrections/safety updates accessible kalir.
- Historical operation record used guide version/date/steps ve later safety corrections'i korur.
- User-fault olmayan guide withdrawal/invalidation selected free-guide replacement hakkini yeniden acabilir.
- Exact wrong-selection correction window validation konusudur; final policy degildir.

### Entitlement change during physical work
- Physical application basladiktan sonra subscription expiry, active-motorcycle switch veya guide entitlement change current work'u paywall ortasinda birakamaz.
- Safe completion veya safe stop/recovery icin gereken butun ilgili steps accessible kalmalidir; yalniz current step yeterli degildir.
- Removed parts'i safe state'e getirme ve critical final checks paywall olamaz.
- Bu continuity yeni paid guide/new work entitlement'i yaratmaz.

### Collaboration downgrade/inactive lifecycle
- Inactive/shared motorcycle prior invitee old contributions'i gorebilir ve historical trust maintenance'a katilabilir; new routine activity durabilir.
- Contributor own evidence withdrawal, own-error correction ve prior contribution dispute-response entitlement'tan bagimsiz kalir.
- Owner revoke authority korunur.
- Premium collaboration sona erse bile actor, timestamp, approvals, evidence status ve correction trail historical record'da kalir.

### Premium/new-activity boundary
- Historical read/proof/correction/safety/recovery core trust'tur.
- Additional simultaneous active motorcycle, new full guide ve new advanced team/coordination flow paid candidate olabilir.
- Large optional media archive icin ayrica commercial retention rules olabilir; historical essential evidence entitlement nedeniyle summary-only hale getirilemez.
- Exact price, subscription structure, quota ve business viability discovery karari degildir.

## 3. Journey deltas

### J-B08-01 — Switch active motorcycle -> inactive trust continuity
1. Free user current active motorcycle'i degistirir.
2. Previous motorcycle routine reminder/new routine activity pasiflesebilir.
3. Previous history/evidence/export/correction/dispute access'i korunur.
4. Past operation'i etkileyen critical safety correction reach-back devam eder.
5. User old motorcycle'i re-activate ederse history/evidence geri gelir; current mileage/use ve physical state yeniden degerlendirilir.

### J-B08-02 — Selected free guide -> complete/reuse/update/withdraw
1. User selection oncesi fit/scope'u gorur.
2. Selected full guide safe end-to-end completion value sunar.
3. Completion yeni free entitlement yaratmaz; selected guide reuse edilebilir.
4. Historical used-version relation korunur.
5. Guide materially corrected/withdrawn ise old work impact/recheck accessible kalir.
6. Guide user fault olmadan unusable olursa replacement selection hakki yeniden acilabilir.

### J-B08-03 — Entitlement loss during active repair
1. Physical work baslamistir.
2. Subscription/active-bike/guide entitlement change olur.
3. New paid activity acilmaz.
4. Current work safe completion veya safe stop/recovery icin required relevant guidance accessible kalir.
5. Critical final checks paywall olmaz.

### J-B08-04 — Premium downgrade -> historical trust preservation
1. User premium'dan free scope'a doner.
2. Bir motorcycle active secilir; other previous motorcycle records read/export accessible kalir.
3. Historical trust metadata/evidence/corrections korunur.
4. Existing started work safe closure alir.
5. New advanced collaboration/new paid guide/new simultaneous active capacity gated olabilir.

- Payment state nedeniyle provenance/evidence/dispute/correction trust bilgisini azaltmak.
- Safety correction/recheck/recovery'yi premium yapmak.
- Physical work basladiktan sonra user'i yarim/sokulmus motorcycle ile paywall'a birakmak.
- Historical contributor attribution/evidence chain'i premium expiry ile eksiltmek.
- Guide completion veya motorcycle switch'i automatic new free-guide credit gibi yorumlamak.
- User-fault olmayan guide invalidation'da historical safety access'i kapatmak.

## 6. Glossary candidates

- **Active Motorcycle** — New routine activity ve normal reminder lifecycle'inin free entitlement icinde yurutuldugu current motorcycle.
- **Inactive Motorcycle** — New routine activity acisindan entitlement disinda olan fakat history/evidence/provenance/correction/safety reach-back erisimini koruyan motorcycle.
- **Historical Trust Maintenance** — New routine activity uretmeden prior record'un dogrulugunu ve trust'ini koruyan correction/dispute/evidence-withdrawal/supporting-evidence islemleri.
- **New Activity** — New routine work/note, new full guide, additional simultaneous active motorcycle veya new advanced collaboration gibi entitlement ile sinirlanabilecek future activity.
- **Safety Reach-Back** — Prior operation/guide later riskli/hatali hale geldiginde entitlement'tan bagimsiz correction, recheck ve safe-recovery access'i.
- **Safe Continuation / Safe Recovery** — Physical work basladiktan sonra entitlement degisse bile motorcycle'i safe result veya safe stop state'e getirmek icin korunmasi gereken guidance.
- **Selected Full Guide Entitlement** — Current working free proposal'da account-level one-full-guide right; completion/bike-switch ile automatic renew olmayan, user-fault olmayan invalidation'da replacement adayi olan entitlement.
