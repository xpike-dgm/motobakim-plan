## Core Semantics

Primary concept:
**precision fit / controlled alignment**

Brand-story connection:
`understand -> align correctly -> apply with control -> build competence`

The mark may communicate:
- modular parts fitting together,
- relationship between knowledge and action,
- technical precision,
- guided progression,
- controlled mechanical understanding.

It must NOT communicate or imply:
- guaranteed repair success,
- certain diagnosis,
- official safety certification,
- racing/performance-first identity,
- service-shop-only positioning.

## Approved Visual-DNA Signals

- precise
- geometric
- modular
- controlled
- restrained angularity
- calm technical credibility
- deliberate rather than fast
- clear alignment
- purposeful spacing
- strong hierarchy

These signals may guide later UI exploration, but the UI must not mechanically copy the logo geometry into every component.

## Required Constraints

1. Preserve precision/alignment more strongly than speed/motion.
2. Avoid increasing rewind/back-arrow or generic navigation-chevron ambiguity.
3. Preserve negative-space separation at small sizes.
4. Do not force K/V letter puzzles at the expense of symbol clarity.
5. Wordmark must preserve clear `r` and both `v` letters in `Kavriva`.
6. Generated Round-02 typography is NOT final typography.
7. Current monochrome form validates direction only; final palette remains open.
8. Current app-icon light/dark examples are presentation studies, not final launcher specs.
9. Trademark uniqueness remains unverified.

## Small-Scale Validation Targets

Before production asset approval, test at minimum:
- 16 px
- 24 px
- 32 px
- 48 px
- 64 px
- current iOS / Android launcher contexts

Watch for internal gaps closing and the mark collapsing into one heavy chevron/arrow shape.
