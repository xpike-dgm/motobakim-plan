## Required Boundaries Preserved

- No uncontrolled `edit -> instantly live in mobile` behavior is authorized.
- Editing, review, approval, release and publication remain distinct states.
- Generated or community material does not become official technical evidence by publication.
- Unknown, insufficient, disputed, stale and recalled states remain truthful outcomes.
- Current meaning and event history remain separate.
- Source withdrawal and role revocation do not silently erase provenance or historical attribution.
- Critical correction, safe stop/recovery and relevant historical access remain protected.
