## Working Authority

### L01 — Community Contribution

Primary job: intentionally convert a personal/private experience into an explicit community contribution.

Working rules:
- sharing is opt-in,
- community content is visibly distinct from official Kavriva guidance,
- optional photos support explanation but are not technical verification,
- what is shared vs what remains private is visible before publication,
- one dominant action is used for sharing,
- no social reputation/ranking mechanics are implied.

### L02 — Contribution Review / Moderation / Appeal

Primary job: understand why a contribution is not currently publishable and what can be done next.

Working rules:
- moderation status and reason are visible,
- repair/revise/resubmit path exists,
- appeal and withdrawal paths remain available where supported,
- publication/acceptance does not mean official technical verification,
- withdrawal must not silently erase dependent provenance/history,
- no fake moderator authority, ranking score, SLA or confidence score is introduced.

## Visual DNA

Preserve:
- near-white canvas,
- graphite/navy typography,
- restrained electric-blue action emphasis,
- amber only for meaningful review/attention state,
- readable type sizes,
- low clutter,
- one dominant job/action per screen.
