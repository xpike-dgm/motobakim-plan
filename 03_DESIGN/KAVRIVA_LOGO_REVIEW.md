## VISUAL QA — OBSERVED

### Symbol

The supplied visual uses a compact monochrome symbol built from several angular geometric modules. The combined silhouette can be read as an abstract K-like construction and also supports the delegated `precision fit / controlled alignment` story.

Strengths:
- Simple enough to work independently from the wordmark.
- Strong monochrome silhouette.
- Natural square-app-icon composition.
- Does not rely on motorcycle silhouette, wrench, gear, shield/check or AI cliches.
- Mechanical / modular character fits Kavriva's `understand -> align -> apply` story.

Main risks:
- Nested angular forms can also be perceived as back/rewind/navigation chevrons.
- Increased slant, repetition or motion treatment could push the mark toward racing/performance branding.
- At very small sizes the internal negative spaces may close and reduce the mark to a generic heavy arrow/chevron.
- K/V interpretation is not self-evident enough to justify forcing additional letter-puzzle complexity.

### Wordmark

In the supplied presentation the word `Kavriva` is readable and the critical `r` and second `v` are visually present.

This is positive for the known naming-confusion risk, but generated typography is not authoritative. A later production wordmark must independently preserve:
- visible `r`,
- visible first and second `v`,
- clean `ri` transition,
- no reading drift toward `Kavira` or `Kaviva`.

### Light / Dark icon samples

The symbol remains recognizable in both shown light and dark icon presentations. This supports continuing with L05A as a direction.

However the current icon background, corner radius, safe area and exact symbol scale are not approved. They are presentation examples only.

## BRAND-FIT QA

### Strong fit
- precise,
- geometric,
- modular,
- controlled,
- technically credible,
- not service-shop-only,
- not racing-first,
- usable without category cliches.

### Balance risk
The current angularity is more technical than friendly. That is acceptable at direction level, but future typography, spacing and UI palette should prevent the brand from becoming cold, aggressive or performance-oriented.

Do not solve this by distorting the symbol into a soft/playful mark; preserve precision and balance approachability elsewhere in the visual system.

## REQUIRED CONSTRAINTS FOR FUTURE REFINEMENT

1. Preserve the `precision / fit / alignment` reading; do not add speed streaks, racing cues or motion exaggeration.
2. Reduce generic rewind/back-arrow ambiguity where possible through geometry, spacing and proportion refinement.
3. Preserve internal negative-space separation at `16, 24, 32, 48, 64 px` validation sizes.
4. Do not force K/V letter tricks if they weaken the symbol.
5. Wordmark must keep `r` and both `v` characters unmistakable.
6. Do not copy the generated typography as final type without a separate type/wordmark selection step.
7. Current monochrome form is the authority for direction; current black/white presentation is not a final palette decision.
8. Do not infer verified/safe/certified semantics from the mark.
9. Do not claim trademark uniqueness from visual QA.
