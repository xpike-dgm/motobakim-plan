## BRAND TRUTH — KORUNACAK

Working brand: **Kavriva**
Suggested pronunciation: `kav-ri-va`

Core brand story:
- understand / kavramak,
- controlled step-by-step application,
- growing competence,
- motorcycle-specific trustworthy guidance,
- mechanical/electrical maintenance + diagnosis + repair + history continuity,
- beginner icin approachable,
- experienced owner icin credible,
- calm and capable, not racing-showoff.

The logo must NOT imply:
- guaranteed repair success,
- certain diagnosis,
- official safety certification,
- professional-service-shop-only positioning.

## CRITICAL LEGIBILITY RULE

`Kavriva` wordmark'inda:
- `r` kaybolmamali,
- ikinci `v` acik secilmeli,
- isim `Kavira` veya `Kaviva` gibi okunmaya tesvik edilmemeli.

Bu, naming validation'dan gelen design constraint'tir.

## AVOID CLICHE-FIRST SOLUTIONS

Default olarak sunlari ana fikir yapma:
- generic motorcycle silhouette,
- wrench + gear,
- shield + checkmark,
- racing flame / speed streak,
- AI sparkle / brain / robot,
- cog icine K harfi gibi ilk-akla-gelen generic kombinasyonlar.

Bunlardan biri ancak gercekten distinctive bir transformation varsa kullanilabilir; sirf category iconography diye kullanma.

## ROUND 01 — FORM-FIRST LOGO EXPLORATION

Amac renk secmek degil, **markanin temel isaret dilini** bulmaktir.

### 1. Once 10 distinct logo concept tanimla

L01-L10 olarak **10 gercekten farkli concept direction** olustur.

Her concept icin kisa olarak:
- Concept ID
- Concept name
- Core metaphor
- Why it fits Kavriva
- Main visual risk
- App-icon potential
- Wordmark relationship

10 concept sadece ayni K harfinin farkli fontlari veya recolor'lari olmamali.

Diversity icin su semantic alanlardan yararlanabilirsin; bunlar zorunlu template degildir:
- grasp / interlock / connection,
- guided progression / step / path,
- mechanical fit / precise alignment,
- modular knowledge -> action,
- continuity / history / loop,
- abstract K/V geometry,
- controlled motion,
- component relationship,
- evidence / clarity without shield-check cliche,
- workshop precision without tool-icon cliche.

### 2. 10 AYRI image uret

Her concept icin **ayri bir image** uret; tek collage/contact sheet yapma.

Image'lar:
- L01 ... L10 olarak acikca tanimlanmali,
- primary olarak monochrome / black-on-light veya equivalent neutral form-study olmali,
- renk wow-effect'i ile zayif formu gizlememeli,
- mobile-app icon olceginde okunabilir sembol dusuncesi gostermeli,
- ayni image icinde mumkunse symbol + `Kavriva` wordmark relationship'i gostermeli,
- presentation mockup'lari logoyu degerlendirmeyi zorlastirmamali.

**Generated typography final production wordmark degildir.** Image model `Kavriva` metnini hatali uretiyorsa bunu saklama; concept'i symbol/form exploration olarak degerlendir ve text accuracy limitation'ini belirt.

### 3. Comparison index

10 image sonrasi compact comparison table ver:
- ID
- Distinctiveness
- Trust/credibility
- Approachability
- Small-icon strength
- Kavriva story fit
- Risk / weakness

Puan gerekiyorsa 1-5 kullanabilirsin fakat puani olculmus user data gibi sunma.

### 4. USER SELECTION

Kullanicidan:
- 1-3 favorite ID,
- her favoride begendigi spesifik parca,
- varsa kesinlikle istemedigi motif/direction
iste.

Kullanici secmeden Round 02'ye gecme.

## ROUND 02 — SELECTED CONCEPT REFINEMENT

User secimi geldikten sonra yalniz secilen concept'leri rafine et.

Her selected concept icin 2-3 refinement uretebilirsin; toplamda gereksiz varyasyon patlamasi yaratma.

Bu turda test et:
- symbol simplification,
- positive/negative space,
- K/V veya abstract structure legibility,
- small app-icon behavior,
- horizontal symbol + wordmark lockup,
- wordmark letter distinction (`r`, second `v`),
- monochrome robustness.

Renk secimi bu turda ancak **secondary exploration** olabilir. Renk final APPROVED brand palette degildir.

User bir refinement'i acikca sectiyse portable handoff package uret.
