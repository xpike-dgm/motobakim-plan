## APPROVED PRODUCT RULES — MUST PRESERVE

### Current meaning before event history

For a historical record, first answer:
**Bu kayit bugun ne anlama geliyor / ne kadar kullanilabilir?**

Then allow deeper provenance/event history on demand.

Do not make the default screen a forensic log dump.

### No silent overwrite / no silent winner

Corrections, disputes, evidence withdrawal or later evidence must not silently erase the prior record/history.

Competing claims may remain visibly unresolved.

### Provenance != certification

Actor, source, evidence, reviewer/context and uncertainty can be shown where relevant, especially in higher-risk/disputed contexts.

Do NOT use a generic shield/check as `officially safe`, `guaranteed correct` or `certified` meaning.

### Evidence withdrawal

A withdrawn evidence source does not automatically delete another independent historical record. Current trust/usable meaning can be re-evaluated while historical provenance remains traceable.

### Critical correction / reach-back

A later correction that affects prior safety/technical work must remain reachable even if the motorcycle is inactive or entitlement changed.

This is NOT a routine reminder. It can legitimately interrupt normal hierarchy and require recheck/remap/safe action.

### Export stays available

Basic history/export/provenance/correction access cannot be retroactively paywalled. Export must preserve enough context to understand records, corrections and provenance.

### Actor role != verification authority

`Kullanici kaydi`, `servis kaydi`, helper/collaborator attribution describe who recorded/performed something. They do NOT automatically mean verified/correct.

## GENERATE EXACTLY 5 NEW MOBILE VISUALS

### I02 — SCR-026 / Historical Record & Provenance

Purpose:
A user opens one history record and understands its **current usable meaning first**, then can inspect evidence/provenance/history.

Required:
- compact motorcycle + record context,
- one clear record title,
- current claim/record meaning near the top,
- actual outcome separated from actor/source/evidence meaning,
- concise provenance/evidence summary,
- correction/dispute state visible when relevant but not giant dashboard treatment,
- deeper `Kaynak / kanit / degisiklik gecmisi` progressive affordance,
- correction/review path where appropriate,
- no trust score percentage,
- no generic `verified = guaranteed` badge.

Use illustrative generic fixture content only; do not fabricate technical values, dates, mileage or model-specific facts.

### I03 — SCR-027 / Record Work — Self / Hobby / External Service

Purpose:
User records real work that happened outside the guided Kavriva flow.

Required:
- what work happened,
- who performed / source role selection where relevant,
- actual outcome including partial/unresolved where needed,
- optional evidence attachment with clear meaning,
- save action,
- no automatic verification because `service` or `professional` is selected,
- no invented date/km/technical values; entry fields can be blank/illustrative placeholders,
- preserve distinction between record creation and maintenance truth/verification.

### I04 — SCR-028 / Correction / Dispute / Evidence Review

Purpose:
User understands competing claims/evidence and can review/correct without silent overwrite.

Required:
- current disagreement/claim state first,
- concise `ne uyusmuyor?`,
- at least two distinguishable claim/evidence sources without declaring a fake winner,
- what is known vs unresolved,
- paths such as `kanit ekle`, `duzeltme oner`, `kendi kanitimi geri cek` where context permits,
- prior disagreement/history remains accessible,
- no courtroom/drama styling,
- no certainty score.

### I05 — SCR-029 / Critical Correction / Safety Reach-Back

Purpose:
A later technical/safety correction affects a prior record/work and the user must understand the impact and required next action.

Required:
- clearly stronger than routine maintenance reminder,
- calm but consequential `onceki kayit/uygulama yeniden kontrol edilmeli` meaning,
- what prior work is affected at a generic level,
- why action is required,
- one dominant recheck/remap/safe next action,
- access to affected record/correction detail,
- must not be hidden behind premium/new-activity gating,
- no fabricated fault, value or severity score,
- not a generic alarm wall.

### I06 — SCR-030 / Data Export

Purpose:
User understands what will be exported and preserves provenance/correction context.

Required:
- export scope summary,
- history/records + evidence/provenance/corrections/disputes meaning represented,
- motorcycle scope clear,
- one dominant `Disari aktar` / equivalent action,
- no premium lock on basic export,
- no implication that export changes/deletes source data,
- no backend/file-format architecture decision unless already explicitly supported; format choices may remain generic.

## CLUTTER RULES

- One primary job per screen.
- Current usable meaning before deep provenance detail.
- Do not make every evidence/source/status a separate giant card.
- Max one dominant CTA zone.
- No trust dashboard, confidence gauge, KPI grid or score wall.
- Timeline/event history should be secondary/progressive except where directly necessary.
- Critical correction may take hierarchy precedence, but still stay calm and actionable.
