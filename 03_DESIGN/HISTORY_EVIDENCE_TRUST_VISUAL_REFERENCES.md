## Approval Boundary

I01–I06 are approved as working Phase-3 visual references for FAM-06 History / Evidence / Trust.

Approval covers hierarchy, current-record meaning, provenance/evidence separation, dispute/correction behavior, critical correction reach-back, export scope and visual continuity. It does NOT finalize production copy, exact claim enums, components/tokens, routing, export format, backend/data architecture, entitlement implementation or Gate 3 -> 4.

## Approved Working Set

- **I01 — SCR-025 History**: existing approved `R04-Gecmis.png` authority; scannable history root.
- **I02 — SCR-026 Historical Record & Provenance**: current usable meaning first; actual outcome separated from actor/source/evidence; deeper provenance progressive.
- **I03 — SCR-027 Record Work — Self / Hobby / External Service**: records real work outside guided Kavriva flow; actor role does not create verification; partial/unresolved outcomes allowed.
- **I04 — SCR-028 Correction / Dispute / Evidence Review**: competing claims shown without silent winner or overwrite; known vs unresolved separated; evidence/correction/withdrawal paths available where applicable.
- **I05 — SCR-029 Critical Correction / Safety Reach-Back**: refined v2 is authority; one consequential message, one affected record, one dominant `Yeniden kontrol et` action; accessible for inactive motorcycles and independent of new-activity entitlement.
- **I06 — SCR-030 Data Export**: motorcycle scope and included history/evidence/provenance/correction/dispute context are clear; export does not alter source records and basic export is not premium-locked.

## Required Constraints

1. Current record/claim meaning appears before raw event history.
2. Actor role, source, evidence and current claim state remain separate semantics.
3. `Servis`, `profesyonel`, contributor or owner attribution does not automatically mean verified/correct.
4. Correction, dispute, new evidence and evidence withdrawal never silently erase prior history.
5. A disputed claim may remain unresolved; no fabricated winner/confidence score.
6. Withdrawn evidence is removed from current evaluation where applicable, while historical provenance remains traceable and independent evidence/records are not automatically deleted.
7. I05 v2 supersedes the first cluttered I05 candidate for this working reference family.
8. Critical correction / safety reach-back is stronger than routine reminder UI and remains reachable for inactive motorcycles / entitlement changes.
9. Basic history, provenance, correction/dispute view and export cannot be retroactively paywalled.
10. Export includes enough context to understand corrections/provenance but does not imply a final file-format/backend architecture.
11. Focused-flow bottom navigation treatment remains PROPOSED ONLY.
12. Exact copy, claim vocabulary, icons, components, tokens, dark mode, accessibility and routing remain non-final.
