## APPROVED PRODUCT RULES — MUST PRESERVE

### Community is opt-in and separate from official guidance

- Community contribution explicit opt-in olmalidir.
- Published personal experience / Q&A, official Kavriva guide veya verified numbered step gibi gorunmemelidir.
- Community content source/actor context and review/moderation state must remain understandable.
- Acceptance/publication does NOT mean technical verification or guaranteed correctness.
- Commercial influence cannot determine technical guidance.

### Personal/private content is not silently published

- A private note, evidence item or personal record must not become community content without an explicit share/publish action.
- Before sharing, user should understand what will be public/shared.
- Sensitive/private material may be excluded; exact privacy implementation remains non-final.

### Moderation is repairable, attributable and appealable

- If a contribution needs changes or is rejected/restricted, show the reason.
- Provide a repair/revise/resubmit path where applicable.
- Appeal path may exist where product rules support it.
- User may withdraw their own contribution/evidence where allowed.
- Withdrawal does not silently rewrite dependent history or past provenance; downstream trust re-evaluation can be required.
- Moderation status is not a technical-truth score.

### No social-feed or reputation-system invention

Do NOT invent:
- follower counts,
- likes/upvotes as trust authority,
- reputation score,
- creator verification badge,
- permanent infinite-feed mechanics,
- marketplace/influencer model,
- payment/promotion placement,
- ranking algorithm.

## GENERATE EXACTLY 2 NEW MOBILE VISUALS

### L01 — SCR-035 / Community Contribution
Purpose: user explicitly turns a private/personal experience into an intentional community contribution.

Fixture direction:
- selected motorcycle/context may be shown compactly,
- contribution is clearly labeled `Topluluk deneyimi` / `Kişisel deneyim` or equivalent,
- simple contribution content area: what happened / what user wants to share,
- optional photo/evidence attachment may exist but is not automatic verification,
- clear preview of what will be shared vs kept private,
- explicit note that contribution is separate from official Kavriva guidance,
- one dominant `Toplulukla paylaş` / `İncelemeye gönder` action,
- lower-emphasis cancel/back,
- no public publishing without deliberate action,
- no social engagement counters.

### L02 — SCR-036 / Contribution Review / Moderation / Appeal
Purpose: user understands why a contribution is not currently publishable and what can be done next.

Use one clear illustrative state such as:
`Düzeltme gerekiyor` / `Yayınlanmadı — açıklama gerekli`.

Required:
- status visible but not alarm-wall,
- concise moderation reason,
- show exactly what needs correction at a human-readable level,
- one dominant `Düzenle ve yeniden gönder` action,
- lower-emphasis `İtiraz et` where supported,
- lower-emphasis `Katkıyı geri çek` / withdrawal path,
- statement that publication/acceptance does not mean official guide verification,
- preserve prior submission/change trace conceptually without showing a technical event-log wall,
- no fake moderator identity or invented SLA.
