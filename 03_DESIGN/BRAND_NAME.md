## Approved Working Consumer-Facing Name

**Kavriva**

Suggested pronunciation: `kav-ri-va`.

`Motobakim` / `MotoBakim` repository ve planning context'inde internal project codename olarak kalabilir; consumer-facing brand calismalari bundan sonra `Kavriva` adini kullanir.

## Approval Meaning

User, second-pass naming validation ve main-manager QA sonrasinda `Kavriva` adini bilinen preliminary collision/confusion risklerini kabul ederek acikca onayladi.

Bu approval:
- `Kavriva`yi Phase-3 design icin working consumer-facing brand name yapar,
- logo ve sonraki brand/UI exploration'in bu isimle ilerlemesine izin verir,
- ismi LOCKED yapmaz,
- hukuki trademark clearance sonucu degildir,
- domain/handle ownership veya availability garantisi degildir.

## Product / Brand Rationale

Kavriva icin kullanilan brand logic:
- `kavramak / anlamak` cagrisimi uzerinden understanding -> controlled application -> competence hikayesine uyum,
- maintenance-reminder seviyesine daralmadan diagnosis, repair, learning, history ve trust kapsamina genisleyebilme,
- Turkce telaffuzunun gorece kolay olmasi,
- beginner icin approachable, experienced user icin fazla cocuksu olmayan coined-brand karakteri,
- app icon / wordmark / international expansion icin kisa ve Latin-karakterli bir yapi.

Bu anlam baglantisi brand story'dir; `Kavriva` yerlesik sozluk kelimesi olarak kabul edilmez.

## Known Preliminary Risks — Accepted, Not Resolved

Naming exploration ve main-manager QA su sinyalleri buldu:
- Turkey/software context'inde `Kavira Software` kullanimi,
- `Kavira` mobile/digital brand kullanimi,
- close spelling `KAVIVA` trademark activity,
- hear/write confusion ihtimali: `Kavriva` -> `Kavira` / `Kaviva`.

User bu preliminary riski bilerek working brand name'i onayladi.

Bu bulgular:
- infringement sonucu DEGILDIR,
- registrability sonucu DEGILDIR,
- formal legal opinion DEGILDIR.

- Relevant jurisdictions/classes icin comprehensive trademark similarity clearance.

## Logo Brief Inputs

Logo exploration su semantic girdileri kullanabilir:
- core idea: understand -> controlled application -> competence,
- character: calm, clear, capable, approachable, technically credible,
- mechanical precision + guided progression + continuity,
- `Kavriva` wordmark'inda `r` ve ikinci `v` okunabilir olmali,
- app-icon seviyesinde ayirt edilebilir simple form tercih edilmeli,
- marka guaranteed repair, certain diagnosis veya official safety certification izlenimi vermemeli.

Avoid default/cliche dependence on:
- generic motorcycle silhouette,
- wrench + gear combination,
- shield + checkmark,
- AI sparkle/brain motifs,
- racing/flame imagery unless a later user-approved direction explicitly justifies it.
