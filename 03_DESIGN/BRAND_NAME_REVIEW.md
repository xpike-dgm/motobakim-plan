## 3. Product / brand fit assessment

### INFERRED — strong fit

`Kavriva` supports the product's intended brand story around:
- understanding,
- learning-by-doing,
- controlled application,
- competence growth,
- mechanical/technical credibility without narrowing the product to reminders only.

It is short, pronounceable in Turkish, and does not require Turkish-specific characters.

### Main weakness

The name does not independently communicate `motorcycle`. A descriptive positioning line may be needed in onboarding/store/marketing contexts. Exact tagline is not decided.

## 4. Collision / confusion assessment

Main-manager status:

`PROPOSED_CONDITIONAL — USER FAVORITE / COLLISION RISK`

Reasoning:
- No obvious exact-name collision was established in the delegated research.
- But `Kavira` is active in Turkish software and also exists as a mobile app name.
- `KAVIVA` is another close spelling with trademark activity.
- `Kavriva` can plausibly be mistyped/heard as `Kavira` or `Kaviva`.

This is enough to prevent silent APPROVAL, but not enough to reject the name automatically.

## 5. Decision boundary

Before logo exploration, user must explicitly choose one of:

1. **Approve Kavriva as the working consumer-facing brand name** while accepting that formal trademark/legal clearance and real-user spelling validation remain future validation work.
2. **Reopen naming** and ask for a new shortlist optimized for lower collision/confusion risk.

No logo prompt should be activated before this explicit choice.

## 6. If Kavriva is approved

Logo brief inputs from the delegated handoff can be carried forward as PROPOSED inputs:
- core idea: understanding -> controlled application -> competence,
- character: calm, clear, capable, approachable,
- possible symbolic territory: connection / interlocking parts / `K`,
- typography must keep `r` and second `v` unmistakable,
- avoid implying guaranteed repair, definitive diagnosis or official safety certification.

These are inputs, not approved logo decisions.

## 7. Current recommendation

Main-manager recommendation: **do not reject Kavriva solely from the current evidence, but do not mark it APPROVED without the user's explicit risk-aware confirmation.**

If the user approves it, proceed to logo exploration while keeping legal/trademark clearance as a separate later validation requirement before public launch/brand investment.
